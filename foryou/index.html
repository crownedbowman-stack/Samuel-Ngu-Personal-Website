What Align does for you

01

It grows with you

Your life changes, and so do you. Align keeps up. You check in. You share the talks and notes you choose to share. Align builds a picture of you that keeps growing, not a one-time result. In 21 days, you see your work rhythm: when you do your best work, and when to protect your energy.

02

Who you are, from real assessments

See how you are wired. See how you see and decide. See what your hard times gave you, and where you add the most value. You do not get a label. You get your pattern, named, and one thing to practice.

03

Decisions weighed against what you care about

Bring a choice, a worry or a new direction. Weigh it against your values and where you want to go.

04

Your priorities, kept in view

Align shows you what matters most. That helps you stay on track and move toward your goals. When you are ready, it can take you from clarity, to work, to ownership.

Yours, by design

  • Your information is yours. We do not sell it. We do not use it to train AI models.
  • Align helps you look at your own values. It does not diagnose you. It does not make your choices for you.
  • What you share is used to help you. Nothing else.
  • Align is a tool that works for you. It is not a person, and it does not replace your own judgment. You pick what to share. You make every decision.

Honest about where we are

  • Align is in its pilot stage. We are building it with early users.
  • It reflects what you tell it and what the assessments show. It is not therapy or medical advice. It cannot predict your future.
  • Most of Align is live today. LegacyLab, the last stage, is still being built.

Privacy

Last updated November 14, 2026

This page tells you what we collect when you join the Align360 list for individuals or book a call. It tells you why, and what you can do about it.

In short: we collect only what you give us on the form. We use it to plan the first look and to follow up with you. We do not sell it. You can ask us to remove it at any time.

What we collect

  • Your name, email and phone number
  • One social profile link or handle. We use it only to check that you are a real person
  • What you most want to understand, the time that works best for you, and whether you want to help shape Align as a design partner
  • The date you said yes to hearing from us

We do not ask for payment details. We do not use ad trackers on this page. If you use the share box after you sign up, the message goes from your own email or text app. We do not see it. We do not collect the addresses or numbers you send it to.

Why we collect it

  • To send you the date and details for the first look, and to call you about it if we need to
  • To plan the session around what people want to learn
  • To make sure sign-ups come from real people
  • To follow up about Align360, including a conversation if you ask for one

Who handles it

A few services run this page. They handle your information only to do their job for us. Netlify hosts the page and runs the form. Airtable stores the list. Calendly handles any call you book, under its own privacy policy. An email service sends you the details and updates. We do not sell your information. We do not share it with anyone else for their own marketing.

Your consent and your choices

We email you because you checked the box that asks us to. You can change your mind at any time. Use the unsubscribe link in any email we send. You can also ask to see what we hold about you, to fix it, or to have it deleted. To do that, book a call through the link on this page and tell us. We will act on it quickly.

How long we keep it

We keep it until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We may keep a small note that you opted out, so we do not email you again.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. Only the people who need it can see it. No online service can promise perfect security, so please do not email us sensitive personal information.

Who this is for

This page is for adults. It is not meant for children. If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above.
