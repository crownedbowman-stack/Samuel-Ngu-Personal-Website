ALIGNCareer Connectors demo

One participant, four views

You start as Maya, an operations analyst at Acme who is in Inspired LeadHER. What Maya chooses shows up for her coach, and as counts for her sponsor and the workforce board. All names and numbers are invented.

  1. Participant. Open DesignSuite for job and workforce trends from a government source. Open Career Navigator: each job shows how well it fits the way Maya likes to work. Tap a job to see why. Then open the Program tab, go to Sharing and privacy, and note the five switches. Turn on the nudge, go to My Program, and press “Send now”.
  2. Coach. Notifications has Maya’s nudge. It says only “Reach out to Maya this week.” Open Maya: you see the next meeting, a short story of what shifted, and what Align suggests asking. Back in the Participant view, turn off “My progress” and watch the story shrink. Then open Pathwyz modules and White label.
  3. Employer. Acme sponsors five seats. It sees that seats are in use and group totals across its own people, never a person. Open Org alignment and note that small groups are combined.
  4. Workforce. The board sees participation by program and region, and the same job trends. Counts under five are not shown. Open Doors and turn the coach door off, then look at the Coach view.
  5. Back to Participant. Open Sharing and privacy: Maya is told coach access is off. Her choices are kept, and nobody is notified.
  6. What comes next. The Enterprise and StrategyOS dashboards are shown separately. They are what Career Connectors can introduce to workforce boards and employers. We also build specialized dashboards for other niches.
