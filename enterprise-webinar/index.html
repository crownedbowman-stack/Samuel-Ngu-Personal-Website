Privacy

Last updated November 8, 2026

This explains what we collect when you join the Align360 demo list or book a call, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

In short: we collect only what you give us on the form. We use it to plan the live demo and to follow up with you. We do not sell it, and you can ask us to remove it at any time.

What we collect

  • Your name, work email, company and seniority
  • An optional LinkedIn link and the topics you choose
  • Your answers about what best describes you and how soon you are looking to act
  • When you agreed to hear from us

We do not ask for payment details, and we do not use advertising trackers on this page.

Why we collect it

  • To send you the date and link for the live demo
  • To plan the demo around what the group cares about
  • To follow up about Align360, including a call or a pilot conversation if you ask for one

Who handles it

A few services run this page and handle your information only to provide that service to us: Netlify hosts the page and runs the form, Airtable stores the list, Calendly handles any call you book (under its own privacy policy), and an email service sends the invite and updates. We do not sell your information or share it with anyone else for their own marketing.

Your consent and your choices

We email you because you ticked the box asking us to. You can change your mind at any time. Use the unsubscribe link in any email we send. You can also ask to see what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted, by booking a call through the link on this page and telling us. We will act on it promptly.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We may keep a minimal record that you opted out so that we do not email you again.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and limit access to the people who need it. No online service can promise perfect security, so please do not email us sensitive personal information.

Who this is for

This page and the demo are for working professionals and are not directed at children. If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above.
