What your clients get

You make the plan. Align gets it to them and helps them stick with it.

  • Your meal plans and workouts, right on their phone
  • Healthy habit goals they check off each day
  • Structure: a quick daily check-in and reminders
  • Accountability: you see how they are doing if they share it
  • Mindset: Align shows them why your plan matters, and their Align Concierge checks in on hard weeks
  • Food tips, meal ideas from what they have at home, and an easy way to message you
  • The full Align app. It is theirs to keep.

What Align Health is, and is not

  • It is a tool to help you coach.
  • It is not a medical record.
  • It does not give medical advice.
  • Medical records stay where you keep them now.
  • A version for medical records is coming later. It is not ready yet.
  • We are at the pilot stage. The demo uses sample data and prototype screens.
  • You make the plans. Align never writes a plan for you, and it never sends a message by itself.
  • Your clients' data is not sold. It is not used to train AI models.
  • We are not HIPAA certified. That is why Align Health holds no medical records yet.

Privacy

Last updated November 8, 2026

This explains what we collect when you join the Align Health demo list or book a call, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

In short: we collect only what you give us on the form. We use it to plan the live demo and to follow up with you. We do not sell it, and you can ask us to remove it at any time.

What we collect

  • Your name, email and business name
  • What you do, how many clients you have, and whether you would consider paying for a founding spot
  • The social profiles you choose to share, and anything you tell us would help you most
  • Your preferred demo time, and who told you about this, if you say
  • If you join through someone's link, we note who shared it so they get credit. If others join through your link, we note that for you.
  • When you agreed to hear from us

We do not ask for payment details, and we do not use advertising trackers on this page. We do not collect any information about your clients.

Why we collect it

  • To send you the date and link for the live demo
  • To plan the demo around what the group cares about
  • To follow up about Align Health, including a Founder's Circle conversation if you ask for one

We may look at the public profiles you share to understand your work and your audience.

Who handles it

A few services run this page and handle your information only to provide that service to us: Netlify hosts the page, Airtable stores the list, Calendly handles any call you book (under its own privacy policy), and an email service sends our messages. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

Your consent and your choices

We email you because you ticked the box asking us to. You can change your mind at any time. Use the unsubscribe link in any email we send. You can also ask to see what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted, by booking a call through the link below.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We may keep a minimal record that you opted out so that we do not email you again.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and limit access to the people who need it. No online service can promise perfect security, so please do not send sensitive personal information.

Who this is for

This page and the demo are for working professionals and are not directed at children. If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above.
