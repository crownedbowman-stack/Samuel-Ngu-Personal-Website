What you will see in the demo

Less guessing about your clients. More time actually coaching them.

01

Your caseload, sorted by who needs you

See what is changing for each client, in which direction and by how much, with a suggested next step. No digging through old notes.

02

Clients choose what you see

Each area can be Off, Summary or Detail. People share more when they stay in control, and you get what the work needs.

03

Replies drafted, never sent for you

Align drafts a message from what changed this week. You edit it and press send. Nothing goes out on its own.

04

Sessions that prep themselves

Clients can ask to talk and pick a time. You get a prep sheet built from what they share, and you can speak your notes instead of typing them.

05

Plans that land on their phone

Assign meals, workouts, habits and daily targets. Clients check them off on their side, and you see progress if they share it.

06

A community you can grow

See how your whole practice is trending with no names attached, then send challenges and events that clients choose to join.

What your clients get

  • Daily food guidance from a 30-second check-in
  • A Nutrition Score explained in plain words
  • Meal ideas built from what is already in their kitchen
  • A direct line to you, and an easy way to ask for a session
  • Their own account, which stays theirs to keep

What Align Health is, and is not

  • It is a wellness and coaching tool for you and your clients.
  • It is not a medical record and does not give medical advice.
  • Clinical records stay in the system you already use. A version for practitioners who need records inside Align is planned, not available today.
  • We are at the pilot stage. Screens in the demo are prototypes on demo data.
  • You assign the plans and you send the messages. Align never writes a plan or sends a message for you.
  • Clients choose what you see. Their data is not sold and is not used to train models.
  • We are not HIPAA certified, which is why Align Health holds no clinical records today.

Privacy

Last updated November 8, 2026

This explains what we collect when you join the Align Health demo list or book a call, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

In short: we collect only what you give us on the form. We use it to plan the live demo and to follow up with you. We do not sell it, and you can ask us to remove it at any time.

What we collect

  • Your name, email and, if you share it, your practice name
  • What best describes you and how many active clients you work with
  • The social profiles you choose to share, and anything you tell us would help you most
  • Whether you would like to be considered as a founding practitioner
  • When you agreed to hear from us

We do not ask for payment details, and we do not use advertising trackers on this page. We do not collect any information about your clients.

Why we collect it

  • To send you the date and link for the live demo
  • To plan the demo around what the group cares about
  • To follow up about Align Health, including a founding practitioner conversation if you ask for one

We may look at the public profiles you share to understand your work and your audience.

Who handles it

A few services run this page and handle your information only to provide that service to us: Netlify hosts the page, Airtable stores the list, Calendly handles any call you book (under its own privacy policy), and an email service sends our messages. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

Your consent and your choices

We email you because you ticked the box asking us to. You can change your mind at any time. Use the unsubscribe link in any email we send. You can also ask to see what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted, by booking a call through the link below.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. We may keep a minimal record that you opted out so that we do not email you again.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information and limit access to the people who need it. No online service can promise perfect security, so please do not send sensitive personal information.

Who this is for

This page and the demo are for working professionals and are not directed at children. If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above.
