Why clients follow through

Align tells the story of why your plan matters.

People don't quit plans because they don't care. They quit because the plan feels separate from their life. Align connects the workout, the meal and the sleep to what is actually happening in their week and to the goal they set, so the "why" is always in front of them.

What Align Health is, and is not

  • It is a wellness and coaching tool for you and your clients.
  • It is not a medical record and does not give medical advice.
  • Clinical records stay in the system you already use.
  • A version for practitioners who need records inside Align is planned, not available today.
