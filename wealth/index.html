Advanced Architecture
Financial Resilience & Recession Architecture
There is no single “recession insurance.” But there is a layered system that sophisticated individuals, families, and businesses use to remain resilient when markets shift. This is the system Samuel helps you build — before you need it.
What Individuals & Families Do
6–12 months cash reserves in high-yield accounts
Disability insurance as income floor
Life insurance with living benefits activated
Fixed indexed annuities for downside buffering
Diversified income streams (job + business + passive)
Debt structured on fixed rates, not variable
Umbrella liability coverage
Cash-value policy as accessible reserve
What Businesses Do
6–18 months operating cash reserves
Trade credit insurance (customer non-payment)
Key-person insurance on critical contributors
Fixed-rate debt locked before rate rises
Revenue diversification & customer concentration reduction
D&O, EPLI, and cyber insurance
Buy-sell agreement fully funded
Corporate-owned life insurance (COLI)
